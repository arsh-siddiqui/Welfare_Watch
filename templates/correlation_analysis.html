📝 Methodology Note

Pearson r measures linear relationship between each indicator and composite welfare score. Spearman r confirms robustness to outliers. R² shows percentage of welfare score variation explained. Marginal impact is calculated by holding other indicators constant and applying the formula weights. All analysis is rule-based — no machine learning is used.
