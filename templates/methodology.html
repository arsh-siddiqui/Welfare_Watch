STEP 2 Indicators are normalised to 0–100

Raw data (like school enrollment counts or hospital beds) are converted to a 0–100 scale using min-max normalisation. This ensures all datasets work regardless of unit — whether the input is a percentage, count, or index. A score of 100 means the best-performing area in that indicator.

Normalised Score = (value − min) / (max − min) × 100

STEP 3 Weighted formula produces Welfare Score

Welfare Score =
  (Education & Literacy × 0.40)
 + (Healthcare Access × 0.30)
 + (Food Security × 0.20)
 + (Employment & Income × 0.10)
Example: District with Education=75, Healthcare=60, Food=80, Employment=55:
(75×0.4) + (60×0.3) + (80×0.2) + (55×0.1) = 30 + 18 + 16 + 5.5 = 69.5/100 → ✅ Good
